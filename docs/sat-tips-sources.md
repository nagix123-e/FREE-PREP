# FREE PREP SAT Study Tips

The Home carousel contains 52 original, concise SAT study prompts. They are paraphrased study guidance, not copied instructional text. The app does not present the sources as endorsements or reproduce their copyrighted prose.

## Source set

- [College Board: How to Use Bluebook Practice Tests](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/bluebook): official digital format, pacing, navigation, and test-like practice conditions.
- [College Board: Reading and Writing Section](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test/reading-writing): RW format, domains, passages, and question organization.
- [College Board: Practice and Preparation](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice): official practice, feedback, and skill/domain targeting.
- [Khan Academy: Creating an SAT Prep Plan](https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/digital-sat/x46a758c6%3Adigital-sat-study-plan/x46a758c6%3Acreating-an-sat-prep-plan/a/creating-an-sat-prep-plan): diagnostics, targeted practice, timing, and review.
- [Khan Academy: Calculators on the SAT](https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/v2-sat-math/x0fcc98a58ba3bea7%3Aabout-the-digital-sat/x0fcc98a58ba3bea7%3Apreparing-for-the-sat/a/calculators-on-the-sat): strategic use of the built-in graphing calculator.
- [Khan Academy: Command of Evidence: Quantitative](https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/v2-sat-reading-and-writing/x0148d4045b6f2379%3Ainformation-and-ideas2/x0148d4045b6f2379%3Acommand-of-evidence-quantitative/a/command-of-evidence-quantitative-lesson): reading tables/graphs and matching data to a claim.
- [Princeton Review: SAT Reading and Writing Tips](https://www.princetonreview.com/sat-study/sat-reading-writing-tips): question-type recognition, evidence, tone, grammar, and pacing.
- [Magoosh: SAT Practice Test](https://magoosh.com/sat/sat-practice-test/): timed practice, review of errors, and adapting a study plan.
- [Magoosh: Top 10 SAT Reading and Writing Tips](https://magoosh.com/sat/sat-reading-writing-tips/): passage/question types, context, and grammar.
- [Magoosh: SAT Math Formula Sheet](https://magoosh.com/sat/all/): use of the provided reference sheet.
- [Kaplan: Digital SAT Guide](https://www.kaptest.com/study/sat/digital-sat/): digital tools, format familiarity, and test-taking methodology.

## Tip inventory

The shipped inventory is the typed `SAT_TIPS` array in `src/content/satTips.ts`. It contains 52 source-tagged tips: 6 format/navigation, 8 review/planning, 20 Reading and Writing, and 18 Math. Each item is short enough for the Home header and rotates every 20 seconds.
